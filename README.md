# Plotline

A map for walking a cemetery section and seeing who is buried around you and how they're related.

- **Explore:** tap the map to stand somewhere, drag the blue dot, or walk with the arrow keys (Shift for bigger steps) or the on-screen pad.
- **My location / GPS:** follows your phone's location when you're actually in the section.
- Tap anyone to see the relationship, the chain of people connecting you, the plot, and links to Geni and walking directions.

- **Sign in with Geni** to see how *you* are related to each person. The page asks Geni directly from your
  browser, using your own account; your sign-in and results stay in your browser and are never sent anywhere else.

Grave locations come from Catholic Cemeteries burial records; people from public Geni projects.
No relationships are stored in this repository. Not affiliated with Geni.

The data in `data/` is generated by a separate script from Geni projects; this repository only holds the published page.
